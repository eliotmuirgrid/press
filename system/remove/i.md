# Remove

**The first step to win is remove that which doesn't serve you.**

The first and most crucial step in optimizing any system isn’t to add, but to subtract—eliminate anything that doesn’t serve your purpose.

Most people struggle with this. They cling to what’s comfortable, mistaking sentimentality for wisdom. But true progress demands a willingness to let go.

**Sunk costs**? Irrelevant. Past investments should never dictate your future.

**Team members who drag you down**—not out of malice, but out of complacency or lost fit? Don’t mistake longevity for value. Loyalty is admirable, but performance is essential.

**Romantic relationships past their expiration date**? Don’t let nostalgia imprison you. Mercy isn’t the same as progress.

**Obsolete legacy systems**—technology that once was an asset, now nothing but a liability. Don’t let history set your pace.

**Overpriced, outdated solutions**? They’re not assets. They’re dead weight. Rip out the friction that slows you down.

Optimization isn’t about addition, but subtraction. Be relentless. Remove what does not need to be there—and watch everything else accelerate

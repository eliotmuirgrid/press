# Accenture/Verizon

One of the worst projects I was ever exposed to was working with Accenture and Verizon.

They **love** to work with big dysfunctional corporate or government clients.  They enjoy
being on endless conference calls while they achieve nothing other than a stream of consulting
revenue which frankly is low margin.

I was so excited when I got this client.

I swiftly became very unexcited when reality kicked in and I realized how unmotivated to actually do anything of value these organizations were.

The people in these conversations were not the visionaries that created these companies.

They were employees.  Showing up a paycheck.

Extreme size can create business disadvantage in big way.  We avoid these kinds of clients now.

# Starting

## The Power of Small Beginnings

**Every big achievement starts with a simple step. The key to unlocking system leverage is to start small.**

Ask yourself: What is the essential system I need to make progress right now?

If your goal is to master cameras with mini-computers like Raspberry Pis, don’t get overwhelmed by the complexity. Begin with the basics:

- How do you connect a Raspberry Pi to a regular power supply?
- How do you attach a monitor?
- Where does the keyboard and mouse plug in?

These simple steps lay the foundation. Master them, and suddenly, the bigger challenges start to shrink in comparison.

**Start small. Build from there.**

Momentum isn’t created by leaps and bounds—it comes from steady progress and layered understanding. Each small success compounds, giving you both confidence and leverage.

Remember: Every great journey begins with a single step. This is how you turn curiosity into mastery

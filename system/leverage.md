# Leverage

**Example: Thermostat in a Home Heating System**

In a house, the **thermostat** is a classic leverage point. By making a tiny adjustment to the thermostat setting, you can influence the entire heating (or cooling) system:

- **Small action:** Turn the thermostat one degree higher (or lower).
- **Big effect:** The heating/cooling system works harder (or not), changing the temperature and energy usage throughout the whole house.

**Why is this leverage?**  
A small, intentional change at a specific place in the system (the thermostat) produces a large effect throughout the system. You’re not just experiencing sensitivity; you’re *leveraging* a control point for maximum impact.

# Conversation

The most transformative conversations don’t happen in noisy rooms or crowded meetings—they happen between two people, face to face.

Yet, too many organizations have fallen into the trap of groupthink. In a crowd, authenticity is stifled. People go silent, afraid to stand out. Others hide behind performance, saying what they believe others want to hear. A select few monopolize the discussion, clouding the room with empty talk, while many simply disconnect, their valuable perspectives lost in the noise.

But when just two people meet—really meet—something extraordinary happens. Barriers come down. Truth emerges. Clarification is possible, discomfort can be voiced, and the hard questions—the ones that spark real progress—get asked and answered.

If you want genuine insight, real trust, and honest change, start with a conversation between two people. That’s where breakthrough begins

The best conversations always happen between two people.

Do not cultivate a culture of group think.  

Where people become quiet or they perform like monkeys, speaking nonsense.

With two people it's possible for clarification to occur and uncomfortable ideas to be discussed.

**This is the power of two.**

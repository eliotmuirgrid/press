# A/B Testing: Optimize Your System, Don’t Agonize

A/B Testing isn’t just for software or marketing—it’s a powerful mindset for any process, including making your home exceptionally clean.

## 1. Skip the Endless Comparison
- When choosing between similar, reasonably priced products, avoid analysis paralysis.
- Trust that no single comparison will make or break your outcome.

## 2. Test in Your Actual Environment
- Buy both products (when practical) and put them to work **where you actually need the results**.
- This hands-on approach provides the most relevant feedback.

## 3. Measure What Matters
- Observe results: Which option cleans more effectively for *your* space?
- Take note of any unexpected performance differences.

## 4. Iterate and Improve
- Rotate in new products or techniques based on what you’ve learned.
- Continue refining with each round of testing.

## 5. Build Confidence Through Data
- Every trial adds to your knowledge base.
- Over time, your decisions become faster, easier, and more precise.

## Applying this idea to vacuuming cleaners. 

In the past year, I purchased 8 different vacuum cleaners. Each trial taught me something new—about suction, maneuverability, noise, and build quality. The process itself—not just the “final answer”—is what brought my cleaning standards up to the next level.

It was not just one type of vacuum cleaner.  There were hand held ones, shop vac units.  Robot vacuums and conventional products from Dyson and Mielle which both excelled in different ways.

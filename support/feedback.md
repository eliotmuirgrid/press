# Feedback

**Feedback loops are an essential part of healthy systems.**

This is a core concept we always return to when trying to understand what makes a system healthy and successful. Regardless of scale—whether it's just a small team or a complex technological system—timely and effective feedback is critical.

As your team or organization grows, relying on informal feedback becomes unsustainable. Without good technology to facilitate feedback, crucial information gets lost. Sometimes, it's not even about large teams or organizations. Think about household systems: a solar battery or a cleaning robot powered by an app that only one person checks—if they remember to check at all. In cases like this, the feedback loop is broken, and the whole system suffers.

Unfortunately, our world is full of systems that lack proper feedback loops. This isn’t just inconvenient—it’s a massive problem. Without feedback, small issues become big bottlenecks, and growth is stifled.

We're here to help you identify and improve these critical feedback points. Often, addressing feedback loops is the key to unlocking higher performance and healthier systems—whether in your team, your technology, or even in your home.

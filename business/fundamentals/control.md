# Controlling Payment Processes

Many vendors, particularly disreputable cloud-based providers, make it difficult to stop paying them.

A good technique for a savvy CEO is to consider using modern cash flow technology, such as [WISE](https://wise.com), to pay for online services. This makes it easy to cycle payment cards—you can create up to five virtual Visa cards, which allows you to easily swap or deactivate cards and stop paying for services you no longer wish to use.

This is often much easier than navigating the deliberately confusing user interfaces of many online vendors, which are designed to make it nearly impossible to cancel their subpar services.

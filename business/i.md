# Billing is Paused

## New Business Model

iNTERFACEWARE used to be a traditional corporation but I think it will soon
become obvious that for many types of businesses like software businesses which
are heavily intellectual property orientated that a better and more profitable
service model is turn the traditional corporation inside out.

## The Core Value of iNTERFACEWARE

The core value of iNTERFACEWARE has always been the same.  There was one highly
gifted and focused developer.  That is me. My name is Eliot Muir and the founder, 
the primary architect and senior developer of iNTERFACEWARE.

The value of business was always very simple.

Iguana 6.1.5 and lately Iguana 6.2.0 were extremely stable products.

In fact global healthcare cannot run without these products. Iguana is so fast
that it has been used in many production contexts where it is simply not replaceable.

It was this fact that made me confident enough to try one of the most audacious
experiments in the history of the technology business.

## The Business Problem

The challenges of moving the product forward under tradition model of a typical
technology corporation made it really difficult to achieve what needed to be done
for the product.

The issue was the presence of 26 or so mostly Canadian employees creately distorted
the necessary communication processes necessary to get a clear view of the needs
of the business.

## Iguana Solves an Extremely Technical Problem

It's an extremely valuable and necessary problem.  People and organizations need reliable data pipes that can easily and securely transform data taking it from one place to another.

This is hugely valuable problem although incredibly poorly understood by almost 100% of humanity.  It's not a terribly sexy problem.  It's a really tough engineering problem where the
issues not easy to understand.

Let's just be honest.

It takes a very focused genius engineer to have the perseverence to do what is necessary.  Some would say it takes an insane genius.  Perhaps they are right,  Results matter.  This problem has not been solved.  Tranditional business models for middleware keep on failing.

## Traditional Business Models for Middle Fail

At one end of the spectrum you have 'abandonware' or open source products like the successor of the Mirth engine.  There are too many cooks in the kitchen and they are using complicated thechnologies which simply cannot be proven mathematically to be secure.

Unfortunately code is not politically aware.  It does not read the fine print in legal service level agreements.  It's one of two things:

 - Correct stable boring code.
 - Incorrect unstable 'exciting' code.

Exciting is a not a feature that anyone particularly wants in their data pipes.

But how do you achieve boring, reliable code?

It's diffcult. Let's look at the problems.

## The Problems with a Traditional Model

Firstly you need profoundly skilled engineering to build a stable interface engine. That
is where I excel.

Secondly you need commitment.  This is my life work.  I have always been insanely you could say committed to the technology.  

The excitement of an 'exit' event of exchanging my life work for a few dollars or even a lot of dollars was not interesting.

Name a single middleware company that has been acquired that has transformed the world.

ZERO.

IT DOES NOT HAPPEN.

The is because you need a profoundly honest intellectual process to achieve the

## Traditional Corporations Stop Honest Communication

I had reached the point that I realized the traditional corporate structure was a dead
end.  Having employees interact with my customers was stopping the real conversations
I needed to honestly see the true requirements of an amazing solution to the middleware
problem.

The people who had the loudest voices in the old structure were the people with the worst
information.  Managers who had long since forgotten how to code.  People who had no real
idea about the true issues they were solving.

That needed to change.

## Billing processes, Contract Negotiations, Consulting consumed 90% of the communication

It was that bad.  It only became that obvious to me once I took the reigns and started
to the whole picture. 

It was insane.  I couldn't believe the reality of what I was seeing.  The truth is
often stranger that we think.

## Bugs in Iguana X consumed the rest

Unfortunately knowing how to deliver stable easy to use product means there is a need
to see the whole picture.  Because the organization was siloed which was shocking for
what wasn't even that big of a company it wasn't obvious just how bad of product Iguana X
was. It looked nice but it didn't function where it counted.

It was harder to use, a lot less stable. It had dependencies on cloud services. In
every practical metric it was an inferior product to Iguana 6.

It's just a tough problem to solve and it doesn't work well with having a large
team to build and service what is just very technical piece of software.

The team needs to be the smallest it can be.

## The New Model - Open and Trust Based

The development process needed to be much more open.  The technology being used
much more lightweight.  You can read about the techniques we are using in this
new website.

But critical infra-structure technology needs to be much visible. You cannot trust
any single organization to "do the right thing".  It has be visible. It has to open.

As customer you have to be able to see the source code and trust that when you compile
it that it's secure that you can trust it like your life will depend on it.

Because pretty soon people's lives will depend on this technology.  It's just going to
be a fact.  Don't shoot the messenger.


## The Security Landscape

We are facing an existential challenge with our global IT infra-structure.  We are in
the early parts of the storm.  Attackers on IT infra-structure will get exponentially
more capable.  That means any imperfection in infra-structure software, **can and will be
exploited**.

That is my vision moving forward.

I don't care if I lose 95% of my existing customers. When the music stops, the
only viable solution for safe middleware pipes will be by company. 

Is that an intense vision? Yes it is.  But did you think the force behind the world's
easiest to use, fastest and most stable integration platform despite not lot of recent
updates wouldn't be an intense personality?

Unfortunately my staff over shadowed my vision.  They was why they needed to go.  At
least in the form they were.  Slowly some are coming back.

## What is happening to billing in the meantime?

Billing processes are paused.  It's not a [bottleneck](/system/toc/bottleneck)   

The 'organization' has enough money to operate for the rest of my life without billing
another single customer. 

But to be clear this is not the plan.  I plan to resume billing once the [Flow](/flowshell)
framework is ready to deliver that infra-structure.

Why is that?  I am finally rigorously applying the [Theory of Constraints](/system/toc) 
framework to the business as a whole. 

I am not planning to achieve everything that needs to be achieved alone.  Yes I am
building some foundational technology which will underpin the whole effort.  But I
also operating through a growing network of affiliated like minded individuals and
organizations.  This will take a team effort. Just not a conventional 'corporate team'.

However if you are a more regulated business and you need more formal support
arrangements I am slowly putting a network together. The problem is the demand

There is just not a lot of legitimate value - that is the issue.

# Business Focus

![](bottleneck.png)

At our core, we recognize that all businesses—regardless of industry or scale—face the same fundamental challenges:

- **Customer Acquisition:** How do we attract and win new customers?
- **Customer Retention and Service:** How do we maintain relationships, communicate effectively, and meet evolving customer needs?
- **Robust Infrastructure:** How do we ensure secure and efficient data flow throughout the organization?
- **Billing:** How do we get paid?

If we intend not just to survive, but to decisively outperform the competition—rather than becoming an overstaffed social safety net for unnecessary 'white-collar knowledge workers'—we must rigorously apply the [Theory of Constraints](/system/toc). This framework enables us to systematically identify and eliminate bottlenecks, unlocking true operational efficiency.

This philosophy was instrumental in the transformation of iNTERFACEWARE. Initially, the process was daunting; we identified that our single greatest constraint was a bloated workforce. Our solution was radical: we eliminated every unnecessary position and rebuilt the business from the ground up.

Today, iNTERFACEWARE operates with an unconventional structure—without any traditional employees. We function as a sole-proprietor-driven company, collaborating with a lean, high-performance network of trusted partners and like-minded individuals. Our core team consists of just four people, sufficient to ensure redundancy in support, while development is managed by leveraging advanced automation and AI for consistency and efficiency.

We operate with a zero-trust security model. Each core contributor is fully accountable for their own infrastructure, minimizing overhead through strategic technology choices and intelligent design.

We consult for a select clientele—only engaging with those who share the drive and mindset to dominate their markets. Our philosophy is unwavering: eliminate unnecessary complexity, maintain relentless focus, and always adhere to the [Theory of Constraints](/system/toc).

This is not just a more efficient way to do business—it is a demonstrably more profitable one. Today, we are on track to achieve over three times the net profit after tax compared to our previous structure. Our operation is proudly run from our home base in the Cayman Islands.

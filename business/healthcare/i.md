## Observations: People and Privacy

Analyzing how these businesses operate reveals an important trend: **privacy concerns are escalating**, especially among professionals in this field. Increasingly, individuals prefer to keep their identities private—a wise move given the growing threat of phishing and AI-driven scams.

**Real-World Insight:**  
I have personally encountered AI-enabled scams that quickly expose vulnerabilities by scraping publicly available data. This underscores the necessity of practicing online caution.

## Why Discretion Matters More Than Ever

- Sharing personal or professional details online used to feel routine.
- However, today’s environment demands a reassessment of what information we share as cyber threats become more sophisticated.
- For the same reasons, we are also considering making this site more private, balancing openness with the need for greater security.

# Iguana 6.2.1

**About Iguana 6.2.1: A Thoughtful Patch Release**

This section of the website highlights the care and consideration going into the Iguana 6.2.1 release. We see this as the first step in showing appreciation and respect to our loyal customers who have placed their trust in us. This release is our way of giving back in the spirit of service.

Our goal is to gradually evolve Iguana 6 and Iguana X, bringing the two products closer together and making them interchangeable without a lot of manual effort on the part of our customers.

A lot of thought is being given to how we can make it easier to migrate between Iguana versions, including allowing [parallel installs](/documentation/iguana/iguana621/installer).

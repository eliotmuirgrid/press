# AI

We are increasingly integrating AI into our own workflows, and we want to make AI—especially locally-run, sovereign AI—accessible directly within Iguana. Our goal is to provide transparent AI solutions that do not lock users into any particular provider, and that give you the choice to preserve your privacy by using local models instead of relying on the cloud.

We be able to do it a way that allows our customers to choose what AI they want and integrate locally.

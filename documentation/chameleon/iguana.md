# Embedded

**Chameleon Now Included with Iguana 6**

Chameleon was our first product and was initially offered as a standalone solution.

During the 2026 restructuring, it became clear that all Chameleon customers were running HL7 over non-encrypted sockets, which was far from ideal.

To address this, we now ship Chameleon together with Iguana, ensuring that all Chameleon customers can finally take advantage of enhanced security — something we have recommended for years.

We encourage you to use Iguana instead. It offers significant improvements and modern must-have features, including the ability to easily encrypt your TCP/IP data streams, thus protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.

You are likely aware of the importance of encrypting this data; in fact, many countries have regulations (often overlooked in healthcare IT) that require it.

Now, with this integrated solution, you have a straightforward way to achieve compliance with minimal effort. There’s no longer any reason to delay — take a moment during your next available downtime to implement these important updates

# Anonymous

One of the innovative features we're developing with [Flow Protect](/flow/protect) is the ability to automatically identify and flag emails sent from anonymous departments—such as accounting, legal, ESG, and other teams that often operate without transparency.

If a business relies on faceless team titles to mask incompetence or poor management, Flow Protect can help bring accountability. Using screen automation and AI agents, it captures and documents evidence of these practices. Then, leveraging [Flow Communicate](/flow/communicate), the system alerts the CEO directly.

We've already started conversations with several frustrated CEOs who are eager to learn how we've reduced our own headcount to a fraction of its previous size.

Clever technologies like [Flow](/flow) make all of this possible. By enabling custom automated workflows, organizations can streamline operations and eliminate dozens—if not hundreds—of unnecessary roles. This kind of automation is exactly what so many organizations need to move forward efficiently

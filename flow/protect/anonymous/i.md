# Anonymous

One of the innovative features we’re building into [Flow Protect](/flow/protect) is the ability to automatically identify and flag emails sent from anonymous departments—such as accounting, legal, ESG, and other teams that often operate without transparency.

Too often, organizations rely on faceless team titles to mask inefficiencies or gaps in management. Flow Protect helps drive accountability by using screen automation and AI agents to capture and document evidence of these practices. Through seamless integration with [Flow Communicate](/flow/communicate), our system notifies the CEO, founder, or business owner directly whenever such issues arise.

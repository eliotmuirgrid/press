# Collaborate

Collaborate with ease. If you are viewing this website and you spot any bad grammar, a broken link, or something that is grossly factually inaccurate, then unlike a traditional website—where you'd email a faceless marketing department—instead you can make a copy of the site, edit it, and offer the changes back to Eliot.

Eliot will review your changes and either say "thanks a bunch," or "bugger off, you're actually trying to make my website worse—just use Flow Publish and make your own website, thanks!"

## How does this work?

You'll need an account on [GitHub](/tconcepts/science/computer/git/github).

Then, look at the source code for the site in this [Git repository](https://github.com/eliotmuirgrid/press).

Try to find the page you are looking for, which will be written in [Markdown](/tconcepts/science/computer/markdown).

It's not too hard to find pages, since Flow Publish simply stores markdown files in folders—it's deliberately simple.

Next, you'll need to [fork](/tconcepts/science/computer/git/fork) the repository so you can have your own copy to make your edits.

The process for forking and editing files is documented [here under forking in GitHub](/tconcepts/science/computer/git/github/fork). If you find yourself pulling your hair out due to inadequate documentation, you're welcome to improve it yourself and send the changes back to Eliot. If it's better, he's likely to approve it.

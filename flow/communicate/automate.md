# Automate

Flow Communicate represents our ongoing effort to automate many of the interactions within our organization that have traditionally relied on expensive and often inefficient human involvement.

We're still refining Flow Communicate, so you may occasionally receive a message that seems out of place. This is simply part of our experimental phase as we learn the most effective ways to deploy our AI technology. Please bear with us as we continue to improve.

Once we have perfected Flow Communicate, we’ll offer it to our clients to help them streamline their own organizations.

Since Flow is open source, you’re never restricted by the features we provide out of the box. You have the flexibility to expand its capabilities, address your organization’s unique requirements, and apply the powerful [Theory of Constraints](/system/toc).

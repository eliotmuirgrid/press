# Stuck

For some read the Mova Home 2 model got stuck all the time on the wooden slats under the olive tree.

Switching it for the Ecovacs robot vacuum seemed to resolve the problem.  That was a very inexpensive
solution requiring little effort.  

The bottlenecks so far seem to be twigs jamming the robot.  The more regularly the robot cleans hopefully
the less we will see of this.

Sometimes just using a different product resolves many problems [see A/B testing](/system/test/ab).

# Stuck

### 1. **Why is my Mova Home 2 Cleaning Robot Getting Stuck?**

These robots rely on wheels, sensors, and navigation algorithms to roam your home. Obstacles—like uneven surfaces, gaps, grooves, or unusual textures—can disrupt their movement or confuse their sensors, causing them to get stuck.

---

### 2. **It Always Gets Stuck on the Brown Wooden Slats with My Olive Tree**

Since the olive tree’s wooden slats are a consistent trouble spot, there’s something about them specifically that creates a unique challenge for your robot.

---

### 3. **What is it About the Brown Wood that Seems to Get this Robot Stuck?**

Here are some possible characteristics of the slats causing trouble:

- **Gaps and Height Difference:** If the slats have spaces between them or are raised above the floor, wheels may get caught or the robot may bottom out.
- **Smoothness vs. Slipperiness:** Wooden surfaces can sometimes be slippery, causing wheels to lose traction and spin ineffectively.
- **Edge Detection:** If the slats are raised, the cliff or edge sensors may think it’s a drop-off, causing the robot to either stop or reverse endlessly.
- **Color and Reflectivity:** Some sensors (especially IR or optical) have difficulty with certain dark colors, glossy surfaces, or low-reflective finishes, confusing navigation.
- **Shape and Arrangement:** Large gaps or narrow slats may mean the robot can’t get good contact with all its wheels, making it teeter or get wedged.

---

### 4. **Potential Reasons**

- **Physical Obstruction:** The wheels physically can’t roll over the slats or gaps.
- **Sensor Confusion:** IR or optical sensors misinterpret the area as dangerous or “empty,” leading to navigation errors.
- **Traction Issues:** Robot slips or spins out on polished wood.
- **Clearance Problem:** Undercarriage or brushes get caught on edges or in gaps.
- **Design Flaw:** The robot model may have trouble with certain flooring transitions by default.

---

### 5. **How Can We Test Which Reason is Real?**

1. **Visual Inspection:**
   - Watch the robot try to cross the slats. 
   - Note exactly where/how it gets stuck: are wheels spinning? Is it not moving at all? Is it beeping or lighting up with an error?
   
2. **Manual Intervention:**
   - Gently lift and place the robot on top of the slats. Does it move, spin in place, or shut down?
   
3. **Sensor Check:**
   - Cover the cliff sensors with a piece of paper/tape temporarily—**CAREFUL, only if safe to do so**—to see if it’s a sensor issue (do NOT leave unattended as this disables a safety feature).
   
4. **Floor Finish Comparison:**
   - Place the robot on other similar wooden surfaces (if available) to see if it has trouble elsewhere, or if the issue is unique to this spot.
   
5. **Wheel Assessment:**
   - Examine wheels for stuck debris or damage.

---

### 6. **Once We Understand – How Do We Fix It?**

Depending on what you find, possible solutions include:

- **Bridge the Gap:** Use a mat, rug, or custom cut piece of thin wood to overlay the slats during vacuuming, giving the robot a continuous surface.
- **Adjust Robot Settings:** Some robots have an “edge sensitivity” or “surface height” setting—see if your model allows tweaks.
- **Block Off Area:** Use the robot’s virtual walls or boundary markers to exclude the olive tree zone if it can’t be navigated safely.
- **Sensor Tweaks:** If it’s a sensor issue, sometimes cleaning the sensors helps, or using white tape to “trick” them.
- **Rearrange Slats:** If feasible, modify the slat spacing or height so wheels can cross comfortably.
- **Request Support/Update Firmware:** Check if Mova offers guidance or firmware updates for improved navigation.

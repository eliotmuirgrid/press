# Folder

## How Folders Were Inspired by the Real World

- In real life, **folders** are paper covers used to hold and organize loose sheets of paper. For example, teachers, doctors, and office workers use folders to keep important documents together, making it easy to find what they need.
- Computers copied this idea! In the digital world, **folders** are used to organize and store files (like documents, pictures, and records) in one place, just like real paper folders.

## Why Folders Are Important for Patient and Customer Records

- **Organization:** Folders help group related files together, such as all records for one patient or all documents for one customer.
- **Easy to Find:** When records are kept in folders (real or digital), it’s much quicker to locate specific information. You don’t have to search through a big messy pile.
- **Privacy and Security:** Folders can help keep records private. In real life, folders may be kept in locked cabinets, and on computers, folders can be password protected.
- **Efficiency:** Staff can easily add, update, or remove information from the right folder, making the process of keeping records updated much faster and more reliable.

**Summary:**  
Folders in computers work just like real folders in an office. They help organize, protect, and quickly find important information—making them especially important for keeping track of sensitive patient and customer records

# Compliance

**The compliance industry is basically a scam.**

iW has gone through two rounds of security penetration testing. The first time, we used PwC (PricewaterhouseCoopers), which was our first experience with just how meaningless compliance can be.

Anyone who had investments in 2007 learned firsthand just how corrupt the compliance industry is. That was when Moody's gave triple-A ratings to mortgage-backed securities.

When a company pays another company for compliance, and there is no real transparency, this is what you can expect.

This is one of the major reasons Eliot parted ways with the last management team at iW. Eliot can prove, without a doubt, that only mathematics, transparency, and engineering quality can solve the security problem.

Why is that?

## The problem doesn't stop with applications; it goes deep into our operating systems.

At iW, we believe that security will only be fully solved for our customers if we not only implement [Flow](/flow) but also go deep into the operating system that our products run on.

Only by removing all unnecessary tools and system calls—which are not needed to run the specific applications and interfaces our customers require—can security truly be achieved.

We plan to make these security innovations as widely available as possible.

# Key

**Public private key is a critical part of the jigsaw puzzle of maintaining security.**

## Broken down:

- **Public/private key:**  
  This refers to a pair of keys — one is called **public** and one is **private**—used in computer security.

- **Critical part of the jigsaw puzzle:**  
  It means public/private keys are **very important** for security, like a crucial piece of a puzzle.

## In SIMPLE terms:

When you want to keep information safe online (like messages, bank data, etc.), computers use “locks” and “keys” to keep things secure.

- The **public key** is like a lock that anyone can use to lock a box (send you a secret message).
- The **private key** is like the only key that can open that box (read the secret message).

So, using public and private keys is a **very important method** to keep information safe on the internet.

---

**Public/private keys help keep things secure, just like strong locks and keys protect your home.*

# Lua 

**Lua** is a small, simple, and secure dynamic programming language that forms the core of everything we do. Renowned for its fast performance, ease of integration, and lightweight footprint, Lua is widely used for scripting in games, embedded systems, and other applications where efficiency is crucial. Its straightforward syntax and dynamic nature make it accessible for beginners, while still being powerful enough to satisfy advanced users.

In a world where security is paramount, Lua stands out as the only sane choice. Its design emphasizes safety and reliability without sacrificing speed or simplicity. Fast, simple, and secure—what’s not to love?

# Camera

You can get very cheap miniature industrial cameras that can be hooked up to an [ESP32](esp32) or [Raspberry Pi](pi). These setups make for inexpensive but powerful sensors to control and monitor equipment, such as my [solar](solar) battery, air conditioner, and pool pump.

[Industrial Mini Camera on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CNCSFQC1)

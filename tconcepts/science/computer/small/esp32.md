# ESP32

**What is an ESP32**?

The **ESP32** is a **low-cost, low-power microcontroller** with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities. It is developed by **Espressif Systems** and is widely used in hobbyist, educational, and professional Internet of Things (IoT) projects.

**Key Features:**
- **Microcontroller**: Dual-core (sometimes single-core) 32-bit Xtensa LX6 CPUs
- **Clock Speed**: Up to 240 MHz
- **Memory**: RAM and flash storage (varies by model)
- **Analog and Digital I/O**: GPIO pins, ADC, DAC, PWM, UART, SPI, I2C, etc.
- **Peripheral Support**: Sensors, actuators, displays, etc.

---

## Connectivity: **Bluetooth and Wi-Fi**

- **Wi-Fi**:  
  - Yes, the ESP32 has integrated Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n).
  - Allows it to connect to wireless networks, serve as an access point, or serve web pages.

- **Bluetooth**:  
  - Yes, the ESP32 supports both **Bluetooth Classic** and **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**.
  - Makes it compatible with a wide range of Bluetooth devices and applications.


# Fingerbot

What do for devices which don't have a real API but have buttons!

[Amazon Link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B97G3B8G?psc=1&smid=A1IM8KN7ATY3I8)

A fingerbot is a small, smart device designed to physically press buttons on your existing appliances and electronics, even if those devices don't have built-in smart features or APIs for digital control. Whether it’s a coffee maker, light switch, or computer power button, you attach the Fingerbot over the button, and it acts as a remote-controlled mechanical finger. Through a companion app, you can trigger this “finger” to push the button at any time, enabling you to automate or remotely control otherwise non-smart devices without any need for wiring or complex installations.

This approach is especially useful in homes or offices where some devices cannot be upgraded or integrated into modern smart home systems due to a lack of digital interfaces. Instead of replacing perfectly good equipment, the Fingerbot offers a simple way to bring these devices into your smart ecosystem. It’s an affordable, non-invasive solution for bridging the gap between analog interfaces and new-age automation, making everyday routines a little more convenient without significant investment or technical know-how.

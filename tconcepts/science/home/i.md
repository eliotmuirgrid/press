# Home

Home management has all the same problems in a microcosm that a bigger
organization faces.

At iW we regard managing our homes like a business to be significant
for our health and productivity.

We use our homes to study better how our [Flow](/flow) technology can
work to make [Systems](/system) work better.

We try to be meticulous about testing products and finding out what actually
works.  This is how every well run organization should do things.  This
how we drive the requirements for [Flow](/flow).  Everything is connected.

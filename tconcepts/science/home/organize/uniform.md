# Uniform

I try to use very uniform repeatable ways of storing material in my home.

I go for high visibility.

These transparent shoe boxes are great:

https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09WHDWPN5

They aren't that expensive and I use them extensively organize tools,
electrical components, switches, bulbs, cords, vacuum cleaner parts and
so on.

They make it easy to keep projects organized and know where to find your resources.

When you find a storage mechanism you like, buy a lot of it so the manufacturer doesn't frustrate you by going out of business.

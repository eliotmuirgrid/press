# Glue

## What is Glue?  

In basic chemistry, **glue** is a substance that **binds materials together** by forming a bond at their surfaces. It is a common type of **adhesive**.

## Why Does Glue Work?  

**At a high level**, glue works due to the interplay of chemistry and physics at the interfaces between surfaces. The main reasons glue works are:

### **Adhesion**  
This is the ability of the glue to stick to other surfaces. It involves
- **Physical attraction** (such as [van der Waals forces](/tconcepts/science/chemistry/waals.md) between glue molecules and the surfaces.
- **Chemical bonding**: Sometimes, glue forms new chemical bonds (like covalent or ionic bonds) with the material’s surface, depending on the type of glue and material.

### **Cohesion**  
This is the glue sticking to *itself*, forming a continuous film or network. This is because glue molecules are attracted to each other and form a strong internal structure as the glue sets (dries or cures).

### **Setting Mechanisms**  
Glues typically set, or harden, in one of a few ways:
- **Evaporation of a solvent** (like in white glue, where water evaporates and the glue hardens)
- **Cooling** (as in hot glue)
- **Chemical reaction** (such as epoxy, which requires mixing two components that react and harden)

### **Surface Penetration**  
Glue often soaks into tiny pores and crevices on the surface of the materials. When it sets, it physically "locks" the surfaces together in addition to chemical attraction.

---

### Analogy  
Think of glue as both a **double-sided sticky tape** (adhesion) and a **net** that becomes rigid (cohesion) as it dries.

### Summary Table

| Principle   | What It Means              | Why It Matters        |
|-------------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Adhesion    | Glue-to-surface sticking  | Keeps two surfaces together    |
| Cohesion    | Glue-to-itself sticking   | Keeps the glue solid/strong    |
| Setting     | Hardening mechanism       | Makes the bond permanent       |
| Surface penetration | Fills small gaps       | Provides extra mechanical grip |

---

## Summary  
Glue works because its molecules are attracted to both the surfaces being glued (adhesion) and to each other (cohesion), and it forms a solid bridge between things as it dries or reacts

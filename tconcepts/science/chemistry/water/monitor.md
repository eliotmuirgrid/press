# Monitor

From first principles, it’s clear that continuous electronic monitoring of water quality is a huge step up from periodic manual checks. Traditional “test strips”—where you dip the strip and squint at the color—leave a lot to subjective interpretation and gaps in data.

Instead, look for a product that:

- Supports **open wireless protocols** for easy data access,
- **Floats** in the water for hands-off operation,
- Is **solar powered** for sustainable, low-maintenance use.

If you find a good product, **get a backup**. The “power of two” rule applies: always have a spare. Cayman sun is punishing on everything—especially electronics—and the harsh environment will eventually destroy even the best equipment. Plan for your device to fail **not if, but when**.

Having two devices also lets you cross-check readings and spot faults quickly, ensuring your monitoring system stays reliable.

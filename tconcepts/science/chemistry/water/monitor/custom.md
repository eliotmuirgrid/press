# Custom 

**Solutions for Seamless Flow**

By combining [small cameras](/tconcepts/science/computer/small/camera), [compact computers](/tconcepts/science/computer/small), 3D printed housings, and temperature sensors connected through [Flow Device](/flow/device), we can create solutions for virtually any challenge.

**Solve any problem:**

- **Redesign machines:** Tired of robots that demand daily maintenance or appliances that never quite work right? We fix designs, so you never pay extra for basic convenience again.
- **Monitor water quality:** With precise sensors, pool water can be monitored so thoroughly that, by harnessing rainwater, you could bypass expensive central water systems altogether.
- **Manage energy use:** Smart systems enable your house to optimize power consumption and even go off the grid when you want.
- **Upgrade appliances:** Tired of smelly, wasteful coffee machines? We can separate milk and water waste for a cleaner experience.
- **Custom lighting:** From bespoke, easy-to-clean, safe USB-powered lighting, to managing and calibrating diverse fixtures using smart cameras, achieve the lighting you desire.
- **Smart sound management:** Control sound and music throughout your home with unparalleled convenience.
- **Advanced air quality:** Build custom air monitoring systems far superior to standard smoke alarms, protecting your environment more reliably.

Our mission is to eliminate interruptions, bottlenecks, and health hazards—[removing every obstacle](/system/toc/bottleneck) between you and true **flow**. By applying the [Theory of Constraints](/system/toc), we ensure every aspect of your environment just works seamlessly.

Best of all, we empower you with the knowledge and tools to do the same for yourself and your customers.

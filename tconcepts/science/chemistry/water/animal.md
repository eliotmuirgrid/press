# Animal

**Animal life can compromise water quality.**

Animals which chose to come and poop in your water definitely compromise the water quality from
a human perspective.

How do you manage animals?  Well the interesting thing is that when we combine systems level thinking
with multiple open technologies then we can do more.

Imagine a pool monitored by camera.  We can detect animals and move robots and activate sounds to scare
the critters away.  Everything compounds when you have connected systems combined with systems thinking.

This means more environmentally sound systems where we need to use less chemicals like [chlorine](chlorine).

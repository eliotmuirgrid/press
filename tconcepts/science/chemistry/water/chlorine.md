# Chlorine

**A little bit of poison can be a good thing.**

This is to kill bugs but hopefully not you too.

## Swimming Pools

- **Target Free Chlorine Level:**  
  **1.0 to 3.0 parts per million (ppm)**
- **Ideal for Most Pools:**  
  **2.0 ppm** is a good middle target.
- **Why:**  
  Enough to keep the water sanitary but not so much as to cause eye/skin irritation or damage equipment.

## Drinking Water (Municipal Tap)

- **Target Chlorine Residual:**  
  **0.2 to 0.5 ppm** (sometimes up to 1.0 ppm)
- **Regulatory Limit (U.S. EPA):**  
  **Maximum allowed is 4.0 ppm**

---

**Summary Table:**

| Application    | Target Range (ppm) | Ideal Target   |
|----------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Swimming Pool  | 1.0 - 3.0          | ~2.0          |
| Drinking Water | 0.2 - 1.0          | ~0.5          |

---

**Too much chlorine** can irritate eyes, nose, and skin, and can also create unwanted byproducts (like chloramines or trihalomethanes).  
**Too little chlorine** means water isn’t disinfected and harmful microbes can grow.

**Key message:**  
Use only as much as necessary to stay within recommended **target ranges** for your specific application!

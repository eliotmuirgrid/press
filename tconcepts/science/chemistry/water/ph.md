# Balance

**Keeping water pH balance is a key part of water quality.**

**pH balance** tells us if water is **acidic**, **neutral**, or **basic** (alkaline). It’s measured on a scale from 0 to 14:

- **pH 7** is **neutral** (like pure water).
- **Less than 7** is **acidic** (like lemon juice).
- **More than 7** is **basic/alkaline** (like baking soda).

**Why it matters:**  
Keeping water at the right pH balance is important for water quality. If pH is too high or low, it can harm plants, animals, and even pipes and equipment.

**In short:**  
pH balance means keeping water neither too acidic nor too basic—**just right**

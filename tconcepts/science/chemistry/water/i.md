# Water 

**Water is one of the key parts of life.**

> “Water’s precious here. It’s the true wealth of Arrakis.”

Another notable line:

> “A man’s flesh is his own; the water belongs to the tribe.”

These quotes underscore water's immense value and symbolism in the world of *Dune*.

Here at iW, we live on a desert island called the Cayman Islands. Water quality has done more for public health than all of medicine combined.

Control your water.  
Control your health.  
Control your wealth.

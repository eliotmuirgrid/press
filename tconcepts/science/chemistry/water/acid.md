# Acid

**Cyanuric acid is an important aspect of water quality.**

**Cyanuric acid** is a **chemical compound** often used in **swimming pools** to help maintain water quality. It is sometimes referred to as a "pool stabilizer" or "conditioner."

## **What does Cyanuric Acid do?**
- **Stabilizes Chlorine**: Cyanuric acid protects chlorine in pool water from being broken down by ultraviolet (UV) sunlight. Without it, sunlight can quickly reduce chlorine levels, making it harder to keep the water clean.
- **Helps Maintain Disinfection**: By slowing the rate at which chlorine is destroyed, cyanuric acid helps ensure enough chlorine stays in the pool to kill bacteria and algae.

## **How does it work?**
- Cyanuric acid **binds to free chlorine** in the water, shielding it from UV rays.
- It does **not disinfect by itself**—it only helps chlorine last longer.

## **Important Points:**
- **Proper Levels are Important:** Too much cyanuric acid can reduce chlorine’s effectiveness. Ideal pool levels are usually between **30–50 ppm (parts per million)**.
- **Testing is Needed:** Pool owners should test cyanuric acid regularly and adjust as needed.

## Summary 

Cyanuric acid is a pool water additive that stabilizes chlorine, protecting it from sunlight and helping maintain safe and effective pool water.

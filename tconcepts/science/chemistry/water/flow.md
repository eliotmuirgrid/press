# Flow

**Still water is dangerous. Flow is essential.**

To keep your pool healthy, understanding and eliminating anything that disrupts flow is crucial. When water stops moving, your pool can quickly become a pond or even a swamp—definitely not what you want.

One common bottleneck is a dirty filter, which restricts water movement. Learn more about this in [Filters](filter.md).

Another important factor is maintaining the right water level. If the water is too low, it can’t circulate properly due to the pool’s physical design. Always check the water level to keep everything moving smoothly.
